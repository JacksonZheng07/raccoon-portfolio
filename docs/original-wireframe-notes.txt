Jackson Zheng portfolio wireframe — final direction

This wireframe uses the supplied cat, raccoon, gecko, and mascot/reference images.
The lizard is intentionally the landing-page visual; cats and raccoons appear as
supporting visual language throughout the work, notes, and contact sections.

Design decisions based on research:
- Clear visual hierarchy: one opening message, one primary action, then selected work.
- Case-study cards are structured around problem, role, decisions, outcome, and learning.
- Navigation stays familiar and short; filters are optional and progressive.
- Interaction is purposeful: work filters and case-study previews explain the site.
- Responsive behavior keeps the lizard hero, one featured project, and one note visible.
- No AI-generated artwork, generic AI copy, fake dashboard metrics, or excessive pill UI.
